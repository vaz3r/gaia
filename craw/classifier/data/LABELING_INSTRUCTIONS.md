# Torrent Labeling Guidelines

Use exactly one of:
`Movies`, `Television`, `Games`, `Music`, `Applications`, `Anime`, `Documentaries`, `Other`, `Unwanted`

## Rules

### Anime
- Japanese animation or anime content.
- Fansub markers: `[SubsPlease]`, `[Erai-raws]`, `[HorribleSubs]`, `[Judas]`, `[EMBER]`, `[DKB]`, `[AsukaRaws]`, `[Sick-Fansubs]`, `[FFF]`, `[Shiwasu no Okina]`, `[shincaps]`, `[Masu]`.
- Japanese title + episode markers (e.g., `- 01 [1080p]`).
- NOT live-action Western TV even if title sounds Japanese (e.g., Tokyo Vice).

### Games
- Interactive entertainment software.
- Look for: `FitGirl`, `CODEX`, `PLAZA`, `REPACK`, `ISO`, `NSP`, `XCI`, `crack`, `Steam`, game title.
- Even Japanese/anime-styled game titles are Games, not Anime.
- Examples: `TEVI [FitGirl Repack]`, `Wartales [NSP]`, `Dynasty Warriors 9`.

### Television
- Live-action episodic content.
- `SxxExx`, `Season x`, `Complete Series`, daily shows.
- Non-English live-action series are Television, not Anime.
- Do NOT label Documentaries as Television just because they have `SxxExx`.

### Documentaries
- Factual/non-fiction content.
- Keywords: `BBC`, `PBS`, `NOVA`, `Frontline`, `National Geographic`, `NHK`, `documentary`, `60 Minutes`, `history`, `nature`, `biography`.
- Even with episode markers (NOVA S52E18), use Documentaries.

### Applications
- Software, not games.
- `setup.exe`, `keygen`, `patch`, version number, known software (AutoCAD, ChessBase, Microsoft, Adobe).
- If it is a database/tool/professional software, label Applications.

### Music
- Albums, singles, discographies, FLAC/MP3, live recordings, music festivals.
- Sanremo is Music.

### Movies
- Feature films, single video file, title with year, no episode markers.
- Non-English films are still Movies.

### Unwanted
- Explicit adult/hentai/JAV, malware/spam, fake, password-protected archives, clearly misleading.
- Legitimate but low-quality or obscure content is NOT Unwanted. Use Other if uncertain.

### Other
- Ambiguous, mixed, low-signal, or does not fit any above.
